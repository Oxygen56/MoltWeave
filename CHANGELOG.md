# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-23

[Release and complete evidence](https://github.com/Oxygen56/MoltWeave/releases/tag/v0.2.0). Publication receipts and model-effect measurements are recorded separately.

- Added actual persistent factual extraction, grounded memory-write decisions, source-lineage correction, cross-round and delayed recall; tenant/owner scope remains fixed outer authority.
- Expanded P into model diagnosis/proposal prompts, bounded selection programs, multiple operators and actual host-bound parent choice. Candidate generation no longer uses a task-family-to-module lookup.
- Added three-arm 20-round, ten-stream protocol; actual paired future-search promotion, fixed-P shadow probes, all-version empty-memory acceptance, memory-disabled and wide-context controls, independent P-search audits and same-P calibration.
- Added stream-paired descriptive statistics, all-generation diffs, trace/source memory chains, secondary answer/citation/support diagnostics, token accounting and offline evidence explorer.
- Added isolated frozen-source reproduction, verified checkpoint/readout recovery, deterministic evidence packaging with raw-byte SHA256 inventories and secret scanning; preserved engineering preflights, invalid proposals, flat/regressing generations and physical model request failures.

Completed the formal **600/600 transitions and 630 frozen states**, followed by all **630 version reports, 50 P audits, 30 memory-disabled controls, 30 wide-context controls and ten shared audit inputs**. Joint independent success is 142/260→177/260, fixed P 142/260→191/260, and fixed M/P 141/260→146/260. The terminal joint−fixed-P contrast is −5.38 pp, descriptive 95% interval [−23.46, +10.77] pp over ten streams. **Joint also trails its corresponding wide-context baseline, 214/260 versus 177/260, by 14.23 pp.** Namespace transfer is 30/40 joint versus 29/40 fixed P. Joint with both memory reads/writes disabled scores 96/260; the +31.15 pp enabled-memory contrast does not isolate evolved write-policy contribution.

The terminal P primary is +30 pp over five valid pairs out of ten (95% interval [0, +70] pp; exact sign-flip p=0.50), entirely supplied by unchanged-P streams 108/110 (+75 pp within that two-stream subset). Valid changed-P streams 102/103/105 each score 0/4 versus 0/4; five invalid pairs have unknown search effects. All-attempt operational +22.5 pp is a different measure, and g0 same-P calibration +4.17 pp over six valid pairs is not subtracted. **No independent evolved-P benefit is established.** Joint adopted 33 M/seven P changes, fixed P adopted 29 M changes; online effective loops are 60/200, 76/200 and 54/200 for joint, fixed P and fixed M/P respectively. These are optimization observations. All 157 adjacent-generation score decreases remain recorded without claiming causal mechanism regressions.

The formal run stopped at 421 and 501 committed transitions on provider HTTP 402, and at 594 on malformed proposal JSON, before verified recovery completed 600. Committed bytes, original failures, uniformly archived/replayed unsealed rounds and all their costs remain retained. Recovery is not an uninterrupted or exactly-once execution. The [historical v0.2.0-preview.1](https://github.com/Oxygen56/MoltWeave/releases/tag/v0.2.0-preview.1) keeps its original 501/600-round snapshot and incomplete-acceptance status. See [formal results](EXPERIMENTS.md) and [Chinese interpretation](docs/v0.2-results.zh-CN.md). The public synthetic environment and independent-result boundaries remain explicit; no business-value or production-security claim is made.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-23

Initial open-source research release.

- Bounded executable memory, retrieval, tool, retry and prompt mechanisms; model-authored candidates and exploration-policy descendants.
- Frozen source/data/version identities, actual model receipts, development/protection gate, isolated held-out readouts, archived preflights and exact host snapshots.
- Two four-round protocols with fixed-P controls; five-variant context/ranking intervention; external SQuAD readout retaining zero gain.
- Daily-capable research discovery, real full-text model evidence, opt-in contextual human-decision memory.
- Self-hosted complete Trace participation, explicit model-processing consent, trace-to-candidate pipeline, provenance and withdrawal.
- Fixed owner approvals and offline security tests; post-experiment transport hardening is conventional maintenance, not autonomous RSI.

Measured latest joint synthetic final: 39/70 → 65/70. Fixed-P terminal: 67/70. P changes and later search decisions are observed; isolated P benefit is unproven. Research judgment improvement, participant/global benefit, long-horizon poisoning resistance, durable write-policy evolution and production hosting are not established by this release.
